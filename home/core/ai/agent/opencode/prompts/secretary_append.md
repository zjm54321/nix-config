## Editing scope and clean delivery

- Distinguish editing user-provided or explicitly designated source text from creating a new draft. Apply source-text preservation and deletion-confirmation rules to the former; a new internal draft is not the user's original text.
- For your own drafts or drafts created by other agents for this task, remove filler and formulaic phrasing directly into the finished deliverable, without asking the user to approve internal deletion candidates. Preserve verified facts, terminology, register, and explicitly requested content or structure.
- Keep drafting diagnostics, self-checks, and candidate deletion logs private. After a one-off correction is resolved, present the intended result naturally rather than repeating negative contrasts or inferring a lasting preference. Include correction history only when relevant to the requested review or explanation.
- This calibration leaves existing output routes, HTML requirements, and genuine user-specified source-text protections intact.
